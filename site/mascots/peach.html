Before the next move

A little more Peach.

Peach arrives at the board with a list. Then a second list, in case the first list becomes overwhelmed. She knows where every brick is, which is comforting right up until one of them moves. Luckily, Peach is learning that a plan can be a soft place to start—not a cage to stay inside.

Peach is not here to solve the board for you. They are here to make the room feel friendly while you find your way through it.

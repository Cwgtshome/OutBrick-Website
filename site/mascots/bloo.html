A little more Bloo.

Every morning, when the village gates click awake, Bloo is already standing on the brightest square. He calls it a starting line. Peach calls it “a perfectly avoidable place to trip.” Sprout calls it exciting. Bloo calls it Tuesday.

Bloo is not here to solve the board for you. They are here to make the room feel friendly while you find your way through it.

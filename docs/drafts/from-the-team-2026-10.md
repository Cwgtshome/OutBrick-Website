**Title:** From the team: October 2026

Hello everyone,

This is the first of a monthly note from us: what shipped, what we heard from you, and what comes next.

**What shipped**

- **OutBrick 5.1, Slide & Match**, brought all 2,000 boards a new way to play: slide bricks home through the gate of their colour, swap neighbours, and line up three or more. Twelve kinds of board, long and shaped bricks, and VoiceOver slide actions.
- **OutBrick 5.1.1** fixed a tip card that could leave a board seeming frozen after extra moves arrived.
- On the website, the **Help Centre** now explains every screen, menu and setting with real screenshots, in six languages, with a whole shelf on VoiceOver and accessibility.
- The new **Support Centre** lets you fix common problems step by step, see known issues and which update fixes them, follow your own support request, attach screenshots, and find help with a specific level.

**What we heard from you**

- Several of you told us VoiceOver could lose its place on the board. Thank you: those reports led us straight to the cause. A card offering boosters could open over the board while you were reading it. The fix is in **OutBrick 5.1.2**, which we have sent to Apple for review.
- Missions were not counting on Slide & Match boards. That is fixed and comes in an update after 5.1.2.

**What comes next**

- More VoiceOver improvements we found along the way, after 5.1.2.
- A beta group and an accessibility panel: if you would like to try updates early or help us shape accessibility, sign up at www.outbrick.site/support/get-involved.

As always, the full list of known issues is at www.outbrick.site/support/known-issues, and a person on the team reads every message.

— The OutBrick team
